About Me

I love software development having been in this area professionally since 2010. I am also a ham radio operator holding call sign VU3DRU. I am technically curious and believe in practical perfection, using the right technology for the task at hand, and learning by doing.

Below is a short summary of my career.

2025 - 2026

  • I current work as a senior developer in a SaaS product company based in the Nordics. I really love my job as I get to write code and make system design decisions.

2010 - 2025

  • I started as a systems engineer in 2010 with a major Indian IT Services company using .NET and MS SQL Server to build desktop and web applications.

  • Finally, I worked as a technical architect in GenAI, Data Analytics and modernization projects which was a really fun and learning intense phase where I got to work deeply in Cloud, Data Analytics and software architecture.
